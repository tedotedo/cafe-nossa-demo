# Sales & Walk-In Pitch Guide — Cafe Nossa (`cafe-nossa-demo`)

## 1. Digital Opportunity Snapshot (Verified 12 Nov 2026)

* **Business:** Cafe Nossa — Independent Portuguese delicatessen, brunch & speciality coffee shop
* **Location:** `109 High Street, Yarm TS15 9BB` (`01642 640274`)
* **Current Web Presence:** **Zero website** across all major domain extensions.
* **High-Value Domain Alert:** **`cafenossa.co.uk` is confirmed UNREGISTERED on Nominet** as of 12 Nov 2026 (~£10/year to register and protect the brand).
* **The Commercial Gap:**
  1. Cafe Nossa was voted **`#1 Coffee`** on *This Is Yarm* and profiled in *The Northern Echo*, generating constant High Street search demand (`"Cafe Nossa Yarm menu"`, `"Portuguese deli Yarm"`, `"grazing platter Yarm"`).
  2. Relying solely on Instagram locks out customers without Meta accounts (who hit an immediate login wall), older High Street shoppers, and local Yarm offices looking to pre-order *pastéis de nata* boxes, Iberian cheese/charcuterie platters, or Portuguese wine gift hampers.
  3. Visitors searching Google cannot verify exact 7-day opening hours, check brunch/deli categories, or send a structured pre-order enquiry outside busy counter rushes.

*(Best timing: mid-morning lull ~10:45–11:30am or mid-afternoon ~2:30–3:45pm, avoiding the 12:00–1:45pm brunch rush.)*

## 4. Common Objections & Calm Answers

* **"Instagram works fine for us — we’re already busy."**
  * *"That’s exactly why this works so well: it doesn’t try to replace your walk-in coffee trade. Instagram blocks anyone without an account after two scrolls, whereas a clean site captures the higher-margin pre-orders — grazing platters, boxes of pastéis de nata for local offices, and Christmas/birthday Portuguese wine hampers — plus secures `cafenossa.co.uk` before someone else parks it."*
* **"Our deli cheeses and wines rotate all the time — we don't want an out-of-date menu."**
  * *"We keep the deli and wine shelf structured by regional style (Vinho Verde, Douro, Alentejo, Iberian cheeses, charcuterie, conservas) rather than locking in individual bottle vintages that sell out, while keeping core coffee, brunch, and platter box tiers crystal clear."*
* **"We don't want to pay monthly commission to ordering apps."**
  * *"Zero commission. Pre-order enquiries go straight to your chosen shop email (or WhatsApp/phone confirmation), and customers pay on collection at 109 High Street or via your normal card terminal."*
